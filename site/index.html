The research question

More steps do not
guarantee a better answer.

A recurrent model refines hidden states using the same shared weights. An unproductive trajectory can stay unproductive.

Instead of only adding depth or selecting another branch, TEBDİL asks: can a useful update come back?

The invention is update transport, not branch selection.Visit another state. Compute there. Return to A.

What changes inside the model?

Illustrative explanation · not a latent projection

Mathematical operator & implementation boundaries

Let F be one genuine shared recurrent transition, including normalization. Let V(H) = F(H) − H.

H_visit = H_A + α_eff (H_B − H_A)
H_A′ = H_A + (1 − β)V(H_A) + βV(H_visit)

Full-scope formula shown. Trust-region clipping changes α_eff; token masks and projected variants apply additional restrictions.

Unlike direct mixing, the returned state is anchored at H_A, not H_visit or H_B.

Recorded curvature is the full-response residual V(H_visit) − [(1−α_eff)V(H_A)+α_eff V(H_B)]. Scoped transfer may use only part of it. This is not a claim about a geometric basin.

What these results do and do not support
Implemented & observable
Real checkpoint execution, hidden-state interventions, excursion-and-return, heuristic adaptive control, and offline-trained controller inference.
Observed, not established
Some recorded trajectories recover; others do not. Fresh-template results do not establish a general advantage over parallel or mixing baselines.
Statistical limits
Small generated task sets, repeated seeds, exploratory method comparisons, no multiplicity correction. Zero inside a confidence interval means improvement is unresolved, not disproved.
Compute limits
Calls include donor and visit work but are not equal FLOPs for varying sequence lengths. These are not strict compute-matched tests. Missing, inconsistent, and reference-only execution ledgers are flagged; those costs are not verified.

Hashes bind exported files to this snapshot, not to an independent certification. Historical audit reports are preserved unchanged; their pass flags do not override the current ledger warning.
